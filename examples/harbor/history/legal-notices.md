# Legal notices are shown word for word

- **Date:** 2026-09-20
- **Decision:** DEC-0041
- **Decided by:** legal

In the context of **screens where the law requires a notice**, facing summaries written to save space, we
decided that **the notice is shown word for word, before the action it concerns**, and neglected summaries,
tooltips and links, because only the full text was approved, accepting longer screens.

## Notices

**Closing a savings account** (flow: close-account)

> Closing this account is permanent. Any interest earned this month is paid with the final balance.
> Standing transfers from this account stop on the day it closes.

# Close a savings account

A sample product brief, written to try the brief check on. It contains one conflict, one unclear case and
one gap on purpose.

## Goal

Let a customer close a savings account from the app in three screens.

## Requirements

- R1. The customer sees the final balance and where it will be paid.
- R2. A pending standing transfer of €450 to the joint holder is shown.
- R3. A short line explains that closing is permanent.
- R4. Before closing, the customer can tidy their payees. Selected payees are removed in bulk, with an undo
  message so the list stays fast to manage.
- R5. A payee can be archived instead of removed.
- R6. The close button uses the brand red so it stands out.
- R7. Interest is calculated according to the rules of each market.
